Not sendable

7 blocking issues, plus 6 warnings

  • css-flex-gridOutlook for Windows renders with Word and collapses flexbox.
  • inline-svgGmail and Outlook drop SVG. Export a hosted PNG.
  • unrendered-template-literalReaders will see ${user.firstName} as text.
  • img-data-uriGmail blocks data: images, and they push the email toward the clip limit.
  • link-relative-or-locallocalhost works on your machine and nowhere else.

Also blocking: img-missing-alt, missing-unsubscribe.

Screenshot in. Inbox-proof HTML out.

Your agent reads the image. Truvek Design handles everything that breaks in inboxes. The loop runs until the lint passes, with no person in it.

  1. Map the structure

    The agent splits the screenshot into bands: header, hero, product grid, columns, footer. Layout only. No competitor images, copy or marks.

  2. Apply your brand

    design_extract_brand pulls colours, fonts and logo from your own site and sets email-safe fallbacks.

  3. Build for every client

    Outlook VML buttons, ghost tables, columns that stack on phones, dark mode, preheader and a plain-text part. Every choice comes with its reason.

  4. Lint until it passes

    51 rules, each with a line number, the inboxes affected, why it matters and the fix. Then hand off to Comply for the legal check.

Right syntax for where you send from

Personalisation and unsubscribe tags written for each platform, so agents never hand-write AMPscript or Liquid.

  • Your own app

    Resend, SES, Postmark, Nodemailerrender({ first_name, unsubscribe_url })

  • SendGrid, Mailgun

    Handlebars{{#if first_name}}{{first_name}}{{else}}there{{/if}}

  • Salesforce Marketing Cloud

    AMPscript%%unsub_center_url%%

  • Braze

    Liquid{{${set_user_to_unsubscribed_url}}}

  • Klaviyo

    Django-style tags{% unsubscribe_link %}

  • Shopify Messaging

    Liquid{{ unsubscribe_url }}
